## 2. What the market searches for that we do NOT serve (evidence per cluster)

1. **TRS MEHP (Kentucky teachers).** Retired teachers search "TRS MEHP," "Kentucky teachers
   retirement Medicare." Only TRS's own dense PDFs answer (MEHP requires Parts A+B + TRS form;
   under-65 KEHP switches to MEHP at 65; open enrollment Nov 1 - Dec 7). Zero consumer-friendly
   explainers exist. Group MA is 26%+ of KY MA enrollment; teachers are a huge Lexington-area
   cohort.
2. **KPPA / state-local government retirees.** 2026: three options (Medical Only, KPPA Essential,
   KPPA Premium; the latter two are Humana Group MA PPO), contribution capped at $199.94/mo
   scaled by service months. Again: official PDFs only, no plain-English guide anywhere.
3. **"My plan is ending" / non-renewal.** ~2.6M MA enrollees nationwide lose their plan for 2026
   (KFF); queries spike Sept-Dec. Rights: SEP, guaranteed-issue Medigap, and a to-Feb-28 window
   after returning to Original Medicare. National sites cover it generically; nobody maps it to
   Kentucky counties/carriers. Strategy doc opportunity #5.
4. **Medigap prices in Kentucky / KY DOI Shopper's Guide.** Searches: "how much does Plan G cost
   in Kentucky." KY DOI publishes the official 2026 Medicare Supplement Guide with rate tables
   (insurance.ky.gov, MSGuide.2026 published 12/19/2025); consumers find affiliate sites instead.
   Honest ranges from research: KY Medigap spans roughly $47-$446/mo; Plan G commonly ~$150-250;
   Plan N typically $40-50 below Plan G. Strategy doc opportunity (Shopper's Guide walkthrough).
5. **Social Security office Lexington.** Pure local utility query served only by directory-spam
   sites. Facts (verify at publish): 2241 Buena Vista Rd, Suite 110, Lexington 40505; local (866)
   530-7754; M-F 9-4; national 1-800-772-1213. Also fills the thin SS category.
6. **Veterans: VA benefits + Medicare.** National query family with a strong local hook (Lexington
   VA Medical Center). Key facts: VA coverage does NOT count as creditable for Part B (no SEP
   protection; late penalty risk), VA drug coverage IS creditable for Part D; VA encourages
   enrolling in A+B for non-VA hospital access.
7. **Under-65 / disabled Medigap (HB 345).** Covered only as a section inside the birthday-rule
   article. No consumer explainer exists anywhere for "Medicare under 65 disability Kentucky."
   Guaranteed issue in first 6 months of Part B; premium capped at weighted average of 65+ rates.
8. **"Best Medicare Advantage plans Kentucky 2026."** High volume, dominated by NerdWallet/Forbes.
   We cannot ethically/compliantly publish a "best plans" list (CMS marketing rules; no
   superlatives without substantiation), but we CAN serve the intent with a comparison-method
   article (star ratings, MOOP, networks, drug tiers) that outranks on usefulness.
9. **Dual eligible / D-SNP Kentucky.** UHC Dual Complete, Wellcare, CareSource, Anthem operate
   D-SNPs in KY; dual-eligibles get a monthly Integrated Care SEP. Ties into existing Extra
   Help/MSP article. Confusing space, scam-adjacent marketing, high need.
10. **kynect/Marketplace to Medicare at 65.** Subsidy ends when Medicare eligibility begins;
    unsubsidized Marketplace premium ($500-900/mo at 64+) vs Part B $202.90. KHBE publishes an
    agent-facing PDF only. Natural fit for the Turning 65 category.
11. **Kentucky Medicaid for nursing homes (2026).** Income limit $2,982/mo single, $2,000 assets,
    spend-down state (MNIL $235/mo), $60 personal needs allowance. We touch this inside the
    nursing-homes article; a dedicated deep-dive competes with elder-law firms. Better as a
    substantial update to the existing article than a new one.

## 4. Rationale highlights

- #1/#2 are the largest untapped local cohorts with literally zero consumer-grade competition
  (only government PDFs). They also seed referral relationships (KRTA chapters, retiree groups).
- #3 is the seasonal play: carrier pullback guarantees displaced-enrollee demand this fall; the
  article must be live before ANOC letters land (late September).
- #4 converts the official KY DOI rate guide into the consumer answer, with the birthday-rule and
  Plan G vs N articles as its internal-link spine. High commercial intent.
- #5 is a utility page: modest volume, weak competition (directory spam), builds SS category and
  local entity signals. Half-day effort.
- #8 deliberately captures "best plans" demand with a compliant method piece instead of a ranked
  list; never name a "best" carrier.
- YMYL discipline: every dollar figure and program rule above was sourced from official pages
  (trs.ky.gov, kyret.ky.gov, insurance.ky.gov, ssa.gov/KFF, chfs.ky.gov) during July 2026
  research, and must be re-verified at writing time; date-stamp all volatile facts.
