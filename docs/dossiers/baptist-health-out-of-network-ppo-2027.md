## 1. The letter (primary source, provided by the owner)

Baptist Health, "Important Information About Your Access to Medical Care from Baptist Health",
dated October 2026, sent to patients seen in the past year whose records show "a Medicare
Advantage PPO plan that does not participate in the Baptist Health provider network."

Verbatim (bold in the original):
> Baptist Health is not scheduling appointments for dates of service beginning Jan. 1, 2027, for
> patients who are enrolled in out-of-network Medicare Advantage plans, which include
> out-of-network Medicare Advantage PPO plans. Exceptions will be made for patients who are
> undergoing an active course of treatment for a serious or complex medical condition.

Reason given: out-of-network plans "impose coverage determinations, prior authorization
requirements, out-of-network restrictions and benefit limitations that are outside of Baptist
Health's control", which "can delay care and hinder Baptist Health's ability to obtain approval".
Scope named: "Baptist Health hospitals, physicians, advanced practice providers, specialists and
outpatient facilities." It points to BaptistHealth.com/MedicareAdvantage for in-network plans.

## 3. Federal rules (confirmed)

| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| PPO: you can use out-of-network providers, usually at higher cost, but "out-of-network doctors, hospitals, and other providers may decide not to treat you, even if you've used them before" | Medicare.gov, "Preferred Provider Organizations (PPOs)" |
| Emergencies: providers must treat you; you are always covered for emergency and urgent care | Medicare.gov PPO page and "Understanding Medicare Advantage Plans" |
| Emergency services out of network at in-network cost sharing; balance-billing limits apply | KFF |
| Annual Enrollment Period 15 Oct to 7 Dec; changes effective 1 Jan | Medicare.gov |
| MA Open Enrollment Period 1 Jan to 31 Mar: one switch to another MA plan or to Original Medicare (with or without Part D), effective the first of the next month | Medicare.gov enrollment-periods booklet; Medicare Rights Center |
| Network-change SEP only when CMS finds a plan's provider terminations during the year significant; a single provider leaving is not enough | Medicare Rights Center SEP chart |
| Medigap in Kentucky: birthday rule (KRS 304.14-525) only for people already on Medigap; leaving MA usually means underwriting; federal trial right within 12 months of first joining MA | our KY birthday-rule and switching articles; KFF |
