Worst-case year: $5,000 plus Part B premiums of $2,434.80 = $7,434.80. Drug costs are a

extras do not count toward the medical maximum.

## 5. Mechanics stated

- The medical deductible, copays and coinsurance for covered Part A and B services in network
  all count toward the in-network maximum. Premiums do not. Part D drug costs do not.
  Supplemental extras (dental, vision, hearing) do not. Out-of-network care on a PPO counts
  toward the larger combined limit.
- Once the maximum is reached, covered medical services are $0 for the rest of the calendar
  year. It resets 1 January.
- Deductible before copays: when a deductible applies to a service, the member pays the
  full allowed charge until the deductible is met, then the copay or coinsurance applies.
