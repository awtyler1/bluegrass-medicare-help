## The three facts that make this page unlike anyone else's

1. **Jessamine County has no full inpatient hospital.** Saint Joseph Jessamine runs the county's
   only full-service 24/7 emergency room, and if you are admitted you are going to Lexington.
   That single fact reorders how a Jessamine resident should choose a Medicare Advantage
   network, and no national site says it.
2. **R.J. Corman Railroad Group is headquartered in Nicholasville.** Railroad retirees enroll in
   Medicare through the **Railroad Retirement Board, not Social Security.** Different agency,
   different process, different phone number. Nowhere else in Central Kentucky does that matter
   as much.
3. **Thomson-Hood Veterans Center in Wilmore** is Kentucky's first state veterans long-term-care
   facility, 285 beds. VA benefits alongside Medicare is a content gap on this site and a real
   question in this county.

## 2. Hospitals and networks (Tier 1)

- **Saint Joseph Jessamine**, 1250 Keene Rd, Nicholasville, (859) 313-1973. **Not a full
  inpatient hospital.** It is a department of Saint Joseph Hospital (CHI Saint Joseph Health /
  CommonSpirit) operating **Jessamine County's only full-service 24/7 emergency room**,
  described as the only freestanding ER in Central Kentucky, plus diagnostic imaging, lab,
  physical rehab and physician offices. Confidence HIGH.
- **Inpatient care means Lexington**: Saint Joseph Hospital / Saint Joseph East, UK HealthCare
  Chandler, Baptist Health Lexington. About 11 to 13 miles, 16 to 22 minutes up the US-27 /
  Nicholasville Road corridor. Confidence HIGH.
- **CHI Saint Joseph Health accepted MA carriers** (undated page): Aetna, Anthem, CareSource,
  Humana, Passport, UnitedHealthcare, WellCare, plus Traditional Medicare. Confidence MEDIUM,
  **page date unknown, may predate 2026. Verify.**

  MA remain out-of-network**; **Humana MA in-network** (restored March/April 2024 after the
  January 2024 break over denials and payment delays). Confidence MEDIUM-HIGH on history,
  MEDIUM on 2026 currency.

  **The employer ranking source is from 2018, get a current table from Jessamine EDA before
  publishing any size claims.**
- **TRS MEHP** (retired teachers): group MA administrator changed from UnitedHealthcare to
  **Humana effective 1 Jan 2026**, automatic enrollment, co-branded cards mailed around Christmas
  2025, Express Scripts still on drugs. Confidence HIGH, primary source `trs.ky.gov`.
- **KPPA** (state/county/city retirees): Humana has administered since 2013. 2026 options are
  Medical Only (not MA, no Part D), KPPA Essential and KPPA Premium (both Humana Group MA PPO
  with Rx). Maximum monthly contribution basis $199.94 at 240+ months, $149.96 at 180-239.
  Confidence HIGH, primary source `kyret.ky.gov`.

- **65+: about 9,800 residents, 17.4%** of the county in 2024 (vs 18.1% Kentucky, 18% US).
  Confidence MEDIUM. Note Jessamine skews slightly *younger* than the state, partly the Asbury
  student population.
- ZIPs: **Nicholasville 40356** (largest, entirely within Jessamine) and 40340 · **Wilmore
  40390** · **Keene 40339**. Confidence HIGH.

  Nicholasville Rd. Traffic on that corridor is a real variable worth mentioning.
- **County-line quirk:** the **Brannon Crossing** area sits at the northern edge of Jessamine,
  on US-27 between Brannon Rd and the Fayette line, about 6 miles from both downtown Lexington
  and downtown Nicholasville, using **Nicholasville 40356 addresses**. Residents there shop and
  see doctors in Lexington but buy Medicare from **Jessamine County's plan list**. Confidence
  HIGH. This is a genuinely useful, decision-changing local detail.
