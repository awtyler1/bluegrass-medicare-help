### A constraint to know before writing

**Scott County's plan profile is identical to Jessamine County's.** Same 37 plans, same 19 at $0,
same $13.55 average, same six carriers. So the plan-count section cannot differentiate Georgetown
from Nicholasville, and writing one from the other's template is exactly the failure this exercise
is meant to end. **Scott's page has to earn its difference somewhere other than the plan menu.**
The two candidates below are where.

## 2. Toyota, and why it is the real story here (Tier 1, item 6)

**Toyota Motor Manufacturing Kentucky in Georgetown is Toyota's largest plant in the world and the
county's anchor employer.** The existing page already gestures at this ("retiring from Toyota or
anywhere else") and then says nothing useful about it. This is the strongest available Scott
County angle, and it is the kind of thing a national writer cannot produce:

- How TMMK retiree medical coverage coordinates with Medicare at 65.
- Whether retirees are moved to a group Medicare Advantage plan, offered a Health Reimbursement
  Arrangement to buy individual coverage, or left to the open market. **These three paths lead to
  completely different advice**, and getting it wrong costs someone real money.
- Whether a retiree on group coverage keeps a guaranteed-issue right to Medigap later.

Confidence **LOW on all of it**. Nothing here is publishable until verified. Do not infer Toyota's
benefit design from other manufacturers. See pull list.

Precedent: the KPPA and TRS retiree guides are among the best-ranking pages on the site. A
Toyota-retiree section would be the same pattern applied to the county's largest employer, and
nobody else covers it.

## 3. Hospital and geography (Tier 1, item 2)

- **Georgetown Community Hospital** serves the county locally.
- Georgetown is roughly 13 miles up I-75 from Lexington, the shortest hop of any county we cover.
  That closeness cuts both ways and is worth testing: it may mean Scott County residents use
  Lexington systems for far more than specialty care, which would make the Lexington network table
  more decision-relevant here than the local hospital's own list.

**Network status for Georgetown Community Hospital is unknown.** It is not a Baptist Health
facility, so the published Baptist payer table does not cover it, and unlike Madison County there
is no shortcut. This requires a phone call. Confidence NONE until then.
