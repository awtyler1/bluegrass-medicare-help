## 7. Lifetime model (our arithmetic; assumptions on the page)

20 years, age 65 to 85. Part B premium excluded (paid on every path).
- Plan G: $160/month at 65, rising 5%, 8% or 10% a year (combines age and price increases;
  KFF's 7% one-year average and the 2026 filings bracket this range).
- Stand-alone drug plan: $35/month (CMS 2026 average $35.09), rising 5%.
- Part B deductible: $283, rising 5% (it was $166 in 2016, about 5.5% a year).
- Result: about $87,000 (5%), $111,000 (8%), $133,000 (10%); per year about $4,300 to $6,700.
- MA-PD: $0 premium (3 in 4 enrollees). Costs more than the Plan G path over 20 years only if
  copays and coinsurance average more than about $4,300 to $6,700 a year, about the average
  plan's entire in-network limit ($5,421).
- One $100,000 year (Medicare-approved outpatient, Part B): Original Medicare alone $20,226
  ($283 + 20% of the rest); MA capped at the plan limit (average $5,421, max $9,250);
  Plan G $283.
- Not knowable from public data: share of MA enrollees who reach their limit (KFF says the
  spending data are not available).

## 9. Plain-language conversions (our arithmetic, shown on the page)

- Rates as "1 in X": 7.7% = 1 in 13; 11.5% = 1 in 9; 80.7% = 4 in 5; 12% = 1 in 8; 13% = 1 in 8;
  8.7% = 1 in 11; 22.7% and 22.9% = nearly 1 in 4; 12% / 15% = 1 in 8 / 1 in 7; 30% = 3 in 10;
  9% = 1 in 11; 46% of 35 million = about 16 million.
- MedPAC $76 billion / about 35 million MA members = about $2,200 per member a year; $11 billion /
  about 62.9 million Part B enrollees (CMS figure used for the $90 rebate) = about $175 a year,
  about $15 a month.
- Medigap: $244 / 1.07 = $228, so the 2025 increase is about $16 a month; $203 to $244 = $41 a
  month, about $490 a year; Plan G $194 / 1.13 = $172, about $22; 12% to 26% of $194 = $23 to $50.
- Against the average check ($2,071 a month, $24,852 a year): $283 = about 4 days; $5,421 = 2.6
  months; $9,250 = 4.5 months; $20,226 = 9.8 months; $86,700 to $133,200 = 3.5 to 5.4 years;
  $6,459 / $17,718 x 12 = 4.4 months of average Social Security income.
