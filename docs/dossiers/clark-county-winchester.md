**Two headline contrasts, both now verified from the CMS file:**

1. **Clark County has 39 Medicare Advantage plans. Fayette has 40.** Nearly the same menu, which

2. **Clark County's MA penetration is 61.72%. Fayette's is 54.53%.** A Winchester retiree is

   national site tells it.

Devoted Health entered Kentucky in 2025 and lists Clark County in its 2026 service area. A new
entrant in a small county is genuinely local news.

## 2. Hospital: Clark Regional Medical Center (Tier 1)

- **79-bed community hospital**, part of **LifePoint Health** (Brentwood, TN), which acquired it
  1 May 2010. Serving east-central Kentucky **since 1917**. New $70M, 131,000 sq ft campus
  opened 2012. All-private rooms; emergency, labor and delivery, diagnostics, and a skilled
  nursing Transitional Care Unit. Confidence HIGH.
- Announced a **$4.6M cardiac cath lab expansion / interventional cardiology program**.
  Announcement date not established, may predate 2025. Confidence MEDIUM, **do not date it**
  until confirmed.
- Address: Hospital Drive, Winchester 40391. Main line found as **(859) 737-8303**: verify.
- **Which MA carriers are in-network at Clark Regional could not be established.** Directory
  listings mention Aetna and Anthem BCBS; a carrier-by-carrier list does not exist publicly.
  Confidence LOW. **This is the single most important phone call for this page.**
- LEX18 reported (March 2025) staff concern over LifePoint parent-company financial cuts at
  Clark Regional. Real local context; use carefully and factually, never as alarm.

## 6. Employers and retiree coverage (Tier 1)

- **Catalent Winchester**: largest private employer in Clark County and the largest
  pharmaceutical/biotech employer in Kentucky. 650+ employees pre-expansion; a $175M expansion
  announced May 2022 added 90,000 sq ft and 277 jobs. **Figures are 2022-dated; current
  headcount unknown.** Confidence HIGH for the facts as of 2022.
- **Infiltrator Water Technologies**: 250-acre campus in Winchester Industrial Park, seven
  buildings, 1M+ sq ft, manufacturing since the early 1990s.
- Also present: Freeman Corporation (wood veneer), Leggett & Platt, Clark County Public Schools,
  Clark Regional Medical Center (300+ employees), city and county government.
- **Retiree coverage that actually applies here:**
  - **TRS MEHP** (retired teachers): group MA administrator changed from UnitedHealthcare to
    **Humana effective 1 Jan 2026**, automatic enrollment, Express Scripts still on drugs.
    Confidence HIGH, primary source `trs.ky.gov` MEHP 2026 Update PDF.
  - **KPPA** (state/county retirees incl. school classified staff): 2026 options are Medical
    Only, KPPA Essential and KPPA Premium (Humana Group MA PPO with Rx); requires Parts A and B;
    max monthly contribution basis $199.94 at 240+ months service. Confidence HIGH, primary
    source `kyret.ky.gov`.

Clark County Public Schools plus county government means TRS and KPPA between them cover a
large share of Winchester's retirees. That is the strongest employer angle for this page.

## 8. Demographics and geography (Tier 1)

- Clark County population **36,972** (2020 census), ~37,900 (2025 est.). Winchester ~19,600.
- **65+: about 7,200 residents, 18.9% to 19.1%** of the county. Confidence HIGH (Census
  QuickFacts).
- **Winchester to Lexington: ~21 miles via I-64/US-60, roughly 30 minutes.** Hospital-specific
  drive times not separately verified.
- ZIPs: 40391 (standard), 40392 (PO box). One directory indicates Clark County also touches
  **40353 (Mount Sterling) and 40509/40515 (Lexington)**. **This matters:** MA availability is
  set by county, not ZIP, so a resident with a Lexington mailing address may still be on Clark
  County's plan list. Verify the cross-county ZIP list before publishing.

## 9. What changed for 2026 (Tier 2)

- **Anthem D-SNPs ended in Kentucky.** Anthem lost its Kentucky Medicaid MCO contract effective
  1 Jan 2025, moved all Kentucky D-SNPs to non-marketed status, and member notices said the plan
  "won't be offered after February 2025." Affected dual-eligibles were moved. Confidence HIGH,
  sources include a CHFS notice PDF. **Still shapes the 2026 market for Clark County duals.**
- **Essence MA out-of-network at Baptist Health from 1 Jan 2026.**
- **Rite Aid Pedro Way gone** (2025 liquidation).
- **Devoted Health** now serving Clark County.
- National 2026 pullbacks: Humana exited 194 counties, UnitedHealthcare 109, Aetna ~100 plans.
  **No source establishes whether Clark County was affected.** Resolve by diffing the CY2025 and
  CY2026 landscape files.
- No Clark Regional / LifePoint carrier dispute found. Absence of evidence, not proof.
