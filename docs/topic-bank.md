# Topic bank (living document)

A standing bank of researched article topics to pull from, split into **national/evergreen** and
**hyper-Kentucky** lists. We mix both: KY pieces build the local moat; national pieces fill
category depth and feed TIG cross-posts. Started July 2026 from live keyword research; add to it
whenever new demand signals appear (client questions, GSC queries, seasonal news), and strike
items when published (move to the "Published" line of `local-seo-content-plan-2026.md` or note
the slug here).

Rules when pulling a topic: verify every fact against primary sources at writing time (facts
noted here were checked July 2026 and go stale), keyword-led title, answer-first opener, no em
dashes, recap + 5-question check + FAQ schema, TIG cross-post when a national angle exists.

9. **Ambulance rides and Medicare** (emergency vs non-emergency, why bills surprise people).
10. **ER vs urgent care vs telehealth on Medicare: what each costs.**
11. **Prior authorization in Medicare Advantage** (what it is, 2026-27 reforms, appeal basics).
12. **How to appeal a Medicare denial** (five levels, deadlines, success rates are high).

14. **Snowbird guide: Medicare in two states** (MA network reality vs Medigap freedom).
