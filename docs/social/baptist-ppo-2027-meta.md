## 1. Facebook post (organic)

Got a letter from Baptist Health about Medicare Advantage? Here is what it means.

Starting January 1, 2027, Baptist Health will not schedule appointments for patients in Medicare Advantage plans that are out of its network. The letter says that includes PPO plans.

For years, a lot of folks in Central Kentucky used a PPO to keep seeing their Baptist doctors, even when their plan had no contract with Baptist. That workaround ends January 1.

What to know:
• Emergencies are still treated and covered.
• Patients in active treatment for a serious or complex condition can ask Baptist about an exception.
• December 7 is the last day to switch to a 2027 plan that includes Baptist.

We wrote a plain-English guide: which insurance companies Baptist lists as in and out of network, what still works, and the options before the deadline.
