# Video shoot priority list (August 2026)

Ranking of all 37 articles for video production, given to Austin for shoot day.
When videos come back: embed in the matching article with VideoObject schema, build a
/videos/ library page, and add each video to the matching TIG cross-post where one exists.
Standards: 3-6 min, direct answer in first 30 seconds, year stated aloud on dated topics.
Seasonal: AEP (#4), plan-ending (#5), and yearly review (#18) must be live by late September.

1. **Turning 65? Your Step-by-Step Medicare Roadmap** - Your flagship funnel topic. The biggest, most-searched audience (people entering Medicare), evergreen for years, and it feeds every landing page and quiz. If you shoot only one video today, shoot this one.
2. **Medicare Advantage vs. Medigap: The Real Difference** - THE decision every client faces, huge evergreen search volume, and the video where an honest local explanation beats every national channel. Core conversion driver.
3. **Does Baptist Health Take Your Medicare Advantage Plan? (2026 Network Status)** - The moat video. No national channel can shoot this, it answers Lexington's #1 question, and it proves you know things call centers don't. Plan to re-shoot each January when networks reset.
4. **Medicare Annual Enrollment Period 2026, Explained in Plain English** - Seasonal rocket: searches explode September through December, and the video needs to be live before October 15. Shoot now so it has time to index. Re-usable each fall with light edits.
5. **Is Your Medicare Advantage Plan Ending? Your Rights and Options in Kentucky** - The other seasonal urgent one. Non-renewal letters land in early October and the people searching are the highest-intent leads of the year. Pairs with the AEP video.
6. **The Kentucky Medigap Birthday Rule, Explained** - A valuable Kentucky right most retirees have never heard of, with essentially zero video competition. Highly shareable ('did you know Kentucky lets you...') and a strong differentiator.
7. **Medicare Made Simple: Parts A, B, C & D** - The classic 'Medicare explained in 5 minutes' video is a perennial top performer and the natural top-of-funnel entry for your whole channel and Learning Center.
8. **Medicare Late Enrollment Penalties: How Much They Cost and How to Avoid Them** - Fear of a lifetime penalty is the strongest motivator in Medicare. Concrete dollar examples ($60.87/month forever) make gripping video and drive people to act.
9. **Working Past 65: Do You Have to Sign Up for Medicare?** - A huge, confused cohort with real money at stake (the 20-employee rule, the HSA trap). Evergreen, and it reaches the professional audience and their adult kids.
10. **Medicare Supplement Plan G vs. Plan N: Which Is Right for You?** - High commercial intent: people searching this are days from buying. A calm side-by-side on camera builds exactly the trust that closes.
11. **Is Medicare Advantage Worth It? An Honest Look** - High-volume opinion search where 'an honest local agent's take' is the whole appeal. Great trust builder; the honesty is the hook.
12. **The 'Medicare Flex Card' for Seniors: What It Really Is** - Myth-bust content hooks hard on video ('that TV ad isn't telling you the truth'). Strong click-through and very shareable with skeptical seniors and their kids.
13. **Medicare Costs in 2026: What You'll Actually Pay** - Simple numbers-on-a-whiteboard video that answers the most basic question. Re-shoot every fall when new numbers publish; becomes an annual franchise.
14. **TRS MEHP: Medicare for Retired Kentucky Teachers** - Zero competition and a passionate, connected audience. One good video shared into KRTA chapters and teacher Facebook groups can travel further than anything national.
15. **When to Sign Up for Medicare: The 7-Month Window** - Short, visual (draw the 7 boxes), and answers a top-searched question in under 3 minutes. Easy win, pairs with the Roadmap video.
16. **Medicare Scams: How to Protect Yourself** - Seniors share warning content more than anything else, and local news picks it up. Positions Austin as protector, not salesman.
17. **Medicare Part D and the $2,100 Cap, Explained** - The cap is genuinely good news most people don't know yet. News-you-can-use video with strong AEP relevance.
18. **Your Yearly Medicare Plan Review (Annual Notice of Change)** - The September video: 'that envelope is not junk mail.' Timely, actionable, and it sells the review habit that brings clients back to you every fall.
19. **15 Common Medicare Myths That Cost People Money** - Bonus format: this one article is a goldmine for fifteen 60-second shorts, one myth each, plus one longer compilation. Best shorts/Reels source in the library.
20. **Free Medicare Help in Lexington, KY (Every Resource)** - The trust video: an agent recommending SHIP and free counselors on camera is disarming and memorable. Great for community groups and local sharing.
21. **KPPA Retirees: Your Three 2026 Options** - Second cohort video, same playbook as the teachers: zero competition, loyal audience, strong referral flywheel through state/county retiree networks.
22. **Medicare Advantage in Central Kentucky: County by County** - 'Your county sets your options' is a surprising, local hook nobody else explains. Supports all the city pages and county searches.
23. **Does Medicare Cover Dental, Vision & Hearing?** - Top-five coverage question by volume. Clear yes/no/how-to-fill-the-gap structure makes an easy, useful video.
24. **What Medicare Doesn't Cover (and How to Fill the Gaps)** - Companion to the dental video; broad appeal and drives the coverage-choice conversation toward a review.
25. **Does Medicare Cover Nursing Homes? The Truth About Long-Term Care** - Heavier topic but deeply searched by worried families; the honest answer builds serious trust with the adult-child audience.
26. **When to Claim Social Security: 62 vs 67 vs 70** - Broadens the channel beyond Medicare to the adjacent question every 60-something asks. Wide appeal, evergreen.
27. **Does Medicare Cover Ozempic, Wegovy & Zepbound?** - Trendy, high-curiosity topic with the 2026 GLP-1 Bridge as genuine news. Good discovery potential; date-stamp it clearly.
28. **Switching from Medicare Advantage to Medigap in Kentucky** - Narrower but high-intent audience; the underwriting trap is a story worth telling on camera. Pairs with the birthday-rule video.
29. **2026 IRMAA Brackets: The Medicare High-Income Surcharge** - Smaller audience but your most valuable clients (higher income, referral-rich). Also resonates with financial advisors who refer.
30. **Help Paying for Medicare: Extra Help and Savings Programs** - Compassion content that serves people who need it most; modest search volume but strong community goodwill and SHIP/agency sharing.
31. **What a Medicare Advisor Really Is** - About-you content: better as a site/About-page video than a search play. Shoot when the camera is warm; keep it short and personal.
32. **Local Broker vs. Captive Agent vs. Call Center** - Good positioning story, but overlaps the advisor video. One of the two is enough for the first wave.
33. **Medicare Enrollment Periods Explained (All Six Windows)** - Useful reference but overlaps the 7-Month Window and AEP videos; a later 'directory' video once the specific ones exist.
34. **2027 Medicare Changes Every Kentuckian Should Know** - Wait to shoot until September when 2027 numbers are final, then it becomes a strong seasonal video paired with AEP.
35. **Medicare in Lexington, KY: A Local Guide** - Broad local overview that the more specific local videos (networks, counties, free help) mostly cover. Low priority as its own video.
36. **Which Lexington Hospitals Take Medicare Advantage? (How Networks Work)** - Superseded on camera by the #3 network-status video; that one carries the same message with sharper specifics.
37. **2026 Medicare Changes Every Kentuckian Should Know** - Aging out mid-cycle; skip in favor of the 2027 edition this fall.
