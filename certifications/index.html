Get certified, one carrier at a time.

Step-by-step instructions to complete each carrier's Medicare certification before you can quote and enroll. Finish these before AEP so you're ready to sell on day one.

Carriers

Work through each carrier's steps in order. A carrier is ready to sell only once every step below shows complete on the carrier's portal.

A
Anthem Blue Cross and Blue Shield Elevance Health · Medicare Advantage & Part D
Active
Portal: GetCertified.ElevanceHealth.com Opens: June 30, 2026 Certifies through: AHIP
Before you start: Certification opens on or after June 30, 2026. Go to GetCertified.ElevanceHealth.com and click “Certify with AHIP” to complete Medicare training and Fraud, Waste, and Abuse (FWA) training. You'll need your access code below.
Access code: External-SelfReg
  1. Sign in and confirm your access code. Sign in and confirm the access code External-SelfReg is displayed. If it does not display, enter the code manually.
  2. Complete AHIP and all compliance training. Finish AHIP Medicare training, Fraud, Waste, and Abuse (FWA) training, and every compliance module.
  3. Sign the attestations. Read and sign each attestation to acknowledge the carrier's rules and requirements.
  4. Complete all product training. Work through every product-specific course so you're certified to quote and enroll Anthem plans.
Start Anthem certification → Opens GetCertified.ElevanceHealth.com in a new tab.

If the portal isn't live yet, check back on or after June 30, 2026.
