Does Baptist Health Take Your Medicare Advantage Plan? Lexington's 2026 Network Status

Austin TylerLocal Kentucky Medicare agent · July 24, 2026 · 9 min read

Here is the short answer, verified against the hospitals' own published payer lists in July 2026: Baptist Health is in-network with Humana, Aetna, and Anthem Medicare Advantage, and out-of-network with UnitedHealthcare, WellCare, and Essence. UK HealthCare's published in-network list is broader and includes UnitedHealthcare and WellCare. That one difference decides where thousands of Lexington-area seniors can affordably get care, and most people find out after they have already enrolled. This article gives you the current carrier-by-carrier picture, where it came from, and exactly how to confirm it for your own plan.

Why you can trust this list (and when not to).Every status below comes from the hospital systems' own published insurance pages, not from a plan brochure or a sales sheet. I checked them in July 2026 and I re-check them every quarter and every January 1, because networks are renegotiated constantly. If you are reading this close to an enrollment decision, use the 10-minute verification steps at the bottom. A list like this is a starting point, never the final word.

First: this only matters if you have (or are considering) Medicare Advantage

Original Medicare has no network. If you have Original Medicare, with or without a Medigap supplement, every hospital in this article accepts you: UK HealthCare, Baptist Health, CHI Saint Joseph, and nearly every doctor in the Bluegrass. You can stop reading and go enjoy your day.

Medicare Advantage plans are private plans with contracts. Whether Baptist or UK is "in-network" depends on whether that hospital and your specific insurer have a signed contract this year. Those contracts are exactly what changed in Lexington, twice, in the last three years.

The UnitedHealthcare and WellCare splits are not new: Baptist ended those contracts in January 2024, citing claim denials and payment delays, and they have not returned as of this writing. The Essence change is new for 2026. Essence Healthcare entered the Lexington market with heavy advertising, and plenty of folks who enrolled did not realize Baptist facilities would leave its network on January 1, 2026.

With UnitedHealthcare or WellCare you are typically in-network at UK but not Baptist. With Essence, Baptist is out. And with Original Medicare plus a Medigap plan, every one of these doors is open.

CHI Saint Joseph: call to confirm

CHI Saint Joseph Health, which operates Saint Joseph Hospital and Saint Joseph East in Lexington plus hospitals across surrounding counties, does not publish a single clean Medicare Advantage status table the way Baptist does. Its insurance page lists accepted plans but notes the list is not complete. The reliable way to check is to call CHI Saint Joseph's line at (844) 303-9355 with your plan name, or use the plan's own provider directory. In my day-to-day work, Saint Joseph contracts with the major carriers serving Fayette County, but I verify it for every client, every year, and you should too.

Why this changes, and why it caught so many neighbors off guard

Hospital-insurer contracts are renegotiated on their own schedules, and either side can walk away. Kentucky has seen more of this lately, not less: carriers trimmed their Medicare Advantage footprints for 2026 (UnitedHealthcare alone left 109 counties nationwide), benefits budgets tightened, and hospitals pushed back on payment practices. The result is that a plan that was fine when you picked it can quietly stop covering your hospital the next January.

You are not powerless about it. Every fall, during the Annual Enrollment Period (October 15 to December 7), you can switch plans for the coming year. Your plan also has to send you an Annual Notice of Change each September. The trap is that most people never read it. A yearly plan review exists precisely to catch a network break before it costs you.

How to verify your own plan in 10 minutes (do this before you enroll or switch)

1Look up your hospital in the plan's provider directoryEvery Medicare Advantage plan has an online "find a provider" tool. Search for the hospital and for each of your doctors by name, including specialists. "Baptist is in" does not automatically mean your specific Baptist physician is.
2Call the plan's member services lineAsk them to confirm your hospital and doctors are in-network for the plan year you care about, and note the date, time, and representative's name. If you are choosing during the fall, ask specifically about the upcoming January 1.
3Call the hospital's billing or managed-care officeAsk, "Are you in-network with [plan name] for 2026?" Directories lag behind real contract changes; the hospital knows its own contracts first. Baptist and UK also keep their insurance pages current, and CHI Saint Joseph answers at (844) 303-9355.

If all three answers agree, you can enroll with confidence. If they disagree, believe the hospital, and pick a different plan or get help sorting it out.

Common questions

Does Baptist Health take UnitedHealthcare Medicare Advantage in 2026?

No. Baptist Health hospitals and Baptist Health Medical Group providers, including Baptist Health Lexington, list UnitedHealthcare Medicare Advantage as out-of-network on Baptist's own published payer table, and have since January 2024. UK HealthCare's published in-network list does include UnitedHealthcare Medicare Advantage, so UHC members in Lexington are typically in-network at UK but not at Baptist. Verified July 2026; always confirm current status before enrolling.

Which Medicare Advantage plans are in-network at Baptist Health Lexington in 2026?

On Baptist Health's own published payer status table (verified July 2026), Humana Medicare Advantage, Aetna Medicare Advantage, and Anthem Medicare Advantage are listed as in-network for Baptist Health hospitals and medical group providers. UnitedHealthcare, WellCare, and Essence Medicare Advantage are listed as out-of-network. Original Medicare and all Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plans are accepted.

Does UK HealthCare take Medicare Advantage plans?

Yes. As of July 2026, UK HealthCare's published in-network insurance list includes Aetna Medicare Advantage, Anthem Medicare Advantage, Humana Choice (PPO) and Humana Gold Choice (PFFS) plans, UnitedHealthcare Medicare Advantage, and WellCare Medicare Advantage. Coverage can vary by specific plan, so confirm your exact plan with UK HealthCare and the insurer before care.

Does Baptist Health accept Original Medicare and Medigap plans?

Yes. Original Medicare has no network, and Baptist Health, UK HealthCare, and CHI Saint Joseph all accept it. Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plans pay alongside Original Medicare, so they are accepted anywhere Original Medicare is. Network worries only apply to Medicare Advantage plans.

What happened between Baptist Health and Essence Medicare Advantage?

Essence Healthcare, a Medicare Advantage insurer that entered the Lexington market, went out-of-network with Baptist Health hospitals and providers effective January 1, 2026, per Baptist's published payer table. Essence members can still use other Lexington systems that contract with Essence, but Baptist facilities are out-of-network.
