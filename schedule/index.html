Before you book

How much does this cost?

Nothing. Agents are paid a commission by the insurance company at rates CMS sets, and that is built into plan pricing whether you use an agent or not. Your premium is the same either way.

What happens on the call?

We go through your doctors and hospital, price your exact prescription list against the plans in your county, and compare your options in plain English.

Will I be pressured to switch?

No. If the plan you have is already the right fit, I will tell you so. Sleep on it, talk it over with your family, and call me back whenever.

Do I have to book online?

Not at all. Call (859) 618-6443 during business hours and you will reach a real person. The calendar is just here for folks who like picking their own time.
