Free Kentucky help beyond an agent

You always have unbiased public options, and a good local agent should point you to every one of them. Here is the whole list, with numbers.

Best for: unbiased answers with zero sales involvement

Kentucky State Health Insurance Assistance Program (SHIP)

SHIP is the state's free, federally funded Medicare counseling program. Its counselors do not sell insurance and do not represent any insurance company, which makes SHIP the gold standard for a second opinion. They help with Medicare questions, Supplement and long-term care insurance questions, billing confusion, and applications for the money-saving programs covered below. Services are free for seniors, people with disabilities, family members, and caregivers.

Statewide: 1-877-293-7447  ·  chfs.ky.gov (search "SHIP")

One honest note about SHIP so your expectations are set: counselors are often volunteers, demand spikes hard every fall, and they explain options rather than manage the paperwork end-to-end. It is a fantastic resource, and around the Annual Enrollment Period you may wait for an appointment.

Best for: connecting to every aging service in Central Kentucky

Bluegrass Area Agency on Aging & Independent Living (BGAAAIL)

Based in Lexington, BGAAAIL is the region's front door for aging services across 17 counties: Anderson, Bourbon, Boyle, Clark, Estill, Fayette, Franklin, Garrard, Harrison, Jessamine, Lincoln, Madison, Mercer, Nicholas, Powell, Scott, and Woodford. It delivers local SHIP counseling and much more: caregiver support, home-delivered meals, homecare programs, and benefits screening. If you are not sure what help exists, start here; the Aging and Disability Resource Center (ADRC) line is the entry point for everything they offer.

Main: (859) 266-1116  ·  ADRC: 866-665-7921  ·  bgaaail.org

Best for: face-to-face help and programs, age 60+

Lexington Senior Center

Fayette County's hub for residents 60 and older, at 195 Life Lane, Lexington, KY 40502 (off Nicholasville Road). Beyond activities and meals, the center connects members to benefits counseling and hosts Medicare education programs, especially in the weeks before fall enrollment. If getting out of the house and talking to a person matters to you, this is the friendly option.

(859) 278-6072  ·  lexingtonky.gov

Every surrounding county has its own senior center too, from the Jessamine County Senior Center in Nicholasville (111 Hoover Drive) to centers in Georgetown, Richmond, and Winchester. BGAAAIL maintains the full list at bgaaail.org, and your county's center is often where SHIP counselors hold local hours.

Best for: official answers, plan comparisons, and enrollment

Medicare.gov and 1-800-MEDICARE

Medicare's own Plan Finder at Medicare.gov shows every Advantage and drug plan sold in your county, and the phone line runs 24/7. When a question is about your specific claims, premiums, or enrollment records, this is the authoritative source. The trade-off: the person answering is reading from the same national screens for a caller in Alaska, and cannot tell you which plans keep you in-network at UK or Baptist.

1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227)  ·  Medicare.gov  ·  TTY 1-877-486-2048

Two more official ones worth having in the drawer: Social Security (1-800-772-1213, ssa.gov) handles your actual Part A and B enrollment and premium billing, and the Kentucky Department of Insurance (insurance.ky.gov) publishes a free annual Medicare Supplement Shopper's Guide with real Kentucky rate comparisons.

If money is tight: free help paying for Medicare

These are not counseling services; they are programs that lower your bill, and applying costs nothing:

  • Extra Help pays most Part D drug plan costs for people with limited income. Apply free at ssa.gov/extrahelp or 1-800-772-1213.
  • Medicare Savings Programs, run through Kentucky Medicaid, can pay your Part B premium (over $200 a month back in your pocket in 2026). Apply through kynect.ky.gov, and BGAAAIL or SHIP will help you with the application for free.

Common questions from Lexington neighbors

Is local Medicare help free in Lexington?

Yes. Working with an independent local agent costs you nothing. The insurance carriers pay the agent the same amount no matter which plan you choose, so there is no fee to you and no reason to steer you.

Will my plan cover UK HealthCare, Baptist, or CHI Saint Joseph?

It depends on the plan. As of 2026, Humana, Aetna, and Anthem Medicare Advantage are accepted at all three Lexington systems, while UnitedHealthcare and WellCare are in-network at UK HealthCare and CHI Saint Joseph but out-of-network at Baptist Health. Networks change year to year, so we confirm your specific hospital and doctors before you enroll. Medicare Supplement plans work differently and are accepted anywhere that takes Medicare.

How many Medicare Advantage plans are available in Fayette County?

Forty for 2026, from seven carriers, and 21 of those have a $0 monthly premium. The carriers are Humana (16 plans), UnitedHealthcare (6), WellCare (5), Anthem (5), Aetna (4), Essence (2) and Devoted Health (2). There are also 25 Special Needs Plans, but eligibility for those is restricted, usually to people who have both Medicare and Medicaid. Source: the CMS CY2026 Medicare Advantage Landscape file, pulled August 2026.

What is Kentucky SHIP and is it really free?

Kentucky SHIP (State Health Insurance Assistance Program) is a federally funded program that provides free, unbiased Medicare counseling to seniors, people with disabilities, family members, and caregivers. SHIP counselors do not sell insurance and do not represent any insurance company. The statewide number is 1-877-293-7447, and locally it is delivered through the Bluegrass Area Agency on Aging network.

What counties does the Bluegrass Area Agency on Aging serve?

The Bluegrass Area Agency on Aging and Independent Living (BGAAAIL), based in Lexington, serves 17 Central Kentucky counties: Anderson, Bourbon, Boyle, Clark, Estill, Fayette, Franklin, Garrard, Harrison, Jessamine, Lincoln, Madison, Mercer, Nicholas, Powell, Scott, and Woodford. Its Aging and Disability Resource Center (866-665-7921) is the entry point for its programs.
