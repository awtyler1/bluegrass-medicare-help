Your first year with Medicare, in four steps

Each step links to the full guide when you want more.

1

Find your seven-month window

Your Initial Enrollment Period is the three months before your birthday month, that month, and the three months after. Sign up in the three months before, and coverage starts the month you turn 65.

2

Sign up now, or safely wait

Still working at an employer with 20 or more employees? You may be able to delay Part B without a penalty. COBRA and retiree coverage do not count, so check before you wait.

3

Choose your path

Original Medicare with a Supplement and a drug plan, or a Medicare Advantage plan. Your first six months on Part B is your window to buy a Supplement without health questions.

Do not skip drug coverage

Common questions

When should I start planning?
About six months before your 65th birthday. Your sign-up window opens three months before your birthday month, and the decisions about Advantage, a Supplement and drug coverage are easier with a few weeks to compare.
What does your help cost?
Nothing, now or ever. Licensed agents are paid by the insurance carriers, and on Medicare Advantage and drug plans that amount is set by Medicare and is the same across carriers, so it never comes out of your pocket and never steers the advice.
Are you part of Medicare or the government?
No. Tyler Insurance Group is a private, licensed, independent insurance agency in Lexington. We are not connected with the federal government or the Medicare program.
What if I am not ready to decide?
Then you do not decide. A review has no obligation. You leave with your dates, your options in plain English, and time to think it over.
