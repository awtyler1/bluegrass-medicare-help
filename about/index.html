Who we are

Our mission is simple: be the place Kentuckians turn to for honest, unbiased Medicare guidance. Not a sales pitch. A resource.

Tyler Insurance Group

An independent Medicare advisory firm, based in Lexington.

We started this firm because too many good people were getting rushed, confused, and sold the wrong thing by someone they would never talk to again. Medicare is one of the biggest decisions you will make for your health and your money. You deserve to understand it, not just sign where someone points.

So we built something simple: a local office that explains things honestly, in plain English, and never charges you for the help. We are independent, which means we work with several insurance companies rather than one, and we compare them for you side by side. If a plan is right for you, we will say so. If it is not, we will tell you that too, even when it costs us the sale. And we are the same people who pick up the phone next year when something changes.

That is the whole mission: to be the place Kentuckians can turn to for honest, unbiased Medicare guidance. Not a sales pitch. A resource.
