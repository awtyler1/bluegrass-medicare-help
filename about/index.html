Meet Austin Tyler, your local Lexington Medicare agent.

A real person in Fayette County who does nothing but Medicare, works for you at no cost, and answers his own phone.

Austin Tyler Licensed Kentucky insurance agent · Founder, Tyler Insurance Group
★★★★★ 5.0 on Google from Kentucky neighbors

Hi, I’m Austin. I’ve done nothing but Medicare since I was 22 — it’s not a side line for me, it’s the whole job. I started Tyler Insurance Group here in Lexington, and Bluegrass Medicare Help is how I reach the Kentuckians I work with every day: folks turning 65, people already on Medicare who want a second look, and adult children helping a parent sort it all out.

If you’ve gotten the mailbox full of Medicare brochures, you already know the problem. Most of what comes at you is a call center in another state reading from a script, or a single company that can only sell you their one plan. I’m the opposite of that: local, independent, and free to use. The insurance companies pay me the same amount no matter which plan you choose, so there’s never a reason to steer you toward anything but the plan that actually fits your doctors, your prescriptions, and your budget.

How I actually help

It usually starts with two lists — your doctors and your prescriptions. From there I compare the major carriers available in your county, check which plans keep your doctors in-network, map every prescription to its drug tier, and walk you through what it really costs. You decide, I handle the paperwork, and then I’m your advisor every year after — the same person who picks up the phone, not a new 1-800 number each fall.

Why local matters in Kentucky

Medicare plans are sold county by county, and the networks at UK HealthCare, Baptist Health Lexington, and CHI Saint Joseph aren’t the same on every plan. A national call center doesn’t know which Lexington specialists take which Advantage plan, or how the options differ in Fayette, Jessamine, Scott, or Madison County. I do, because I live and work here.

Free, and no pressure — really

There’s no fee for my help, no obligation, and no hard sell. If the best move for you is to keep exactly what you have, I’ll tell you that. My whole approach is guide, educate, then help — in that order. That’s the same reason I write the plain-English guides in the Learning Center: the more you understand, the better your decision, whether or not you ever call me.
