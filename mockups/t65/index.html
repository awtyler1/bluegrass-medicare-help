T65 cold landing page: 4 variations

All four use the identical final, compliance-reviewed copy and the same form wiring. Only the design and layout differ. Pick one to promote to the live funnel path.

V1 · The Editorial Letter

Calm, centered single column with thin rust rules between sections. Circular portrait, quietest of the four. Feels like a well-designed magazine essay.
V2 · Split Hero

Two-column hero with the form card fully above the fold on desktop, sticky bottom CTA on mobile. Shortest path from ad click to submitted form.
V3 · The Kitchen Table Letter

The whole page is one paper letter from Austin: polaroid photo with tape, handwritten margin notes, a reply card stapled in as the form. Warmest and most personal.
V4 · Bold Conversion

Biggest typography, full-bleed rust and ink bands, sticky header CTA, timeline steps. The most assertive, high-contrast direct-response treatment.
